How a run goes

  1. Pick a mission in the main menu.
  2. Play the stage. Fight the zombies, open power-up chests and break the cages to free the villagers.
  3. Finish the mission. The chest screen pays out coins and gems the first time you complete it.
  4. Go to the village. The rescued villagers join it. Give them jobs, spend what they make on upgrades, then head back out. Each mission you complete moves the village forward one day.

First launch: a short tutorial. You start empty-handed and find the belt in a chest, and a counter shows how many zombies are left across its waves. Played before? The pause menu has Skip Tutorial.

Belt combat and spam combos

A belt you actually swing

Move with the joystick and swing with one button. The belt is a physics rope: it whips up from under, out in front and over your head. On a hit it knocks zombies back, wraps round the target and flops off.

Spam combo

Tap fast to build heat. With heat your swings get up to 2.5× faster and the slap sound climbs in pitch. A tap during a swing chains straight into the next one. Stop tapping and it cools off.

Stamina

Running and attacking both draw from your stamina bar, so you can't just sprint and swing forever.

Getting hit

You flash red for a moment when you take a hit, in the stages, the tutorial and the village.

Day, night and flashlight

A day/night cycle lights the buildings at night, and your flashlight comes out on its own when it gets dark. Roofs fade away when you walk inside stone buildings.

Everything on screen

Health and stamina bars, a kill counter, an arrow pointing to the exit and a minimap that grows when you tap it.

Loot chests

Every mission also hides 3–6 chests with coins in them, and later missions pay more. About one in six is golden: fewer coins, but 1–2 gems.

Boss: the Log Brute

Waiting at the end of mission 5. It marks a spot with a red light, then slams its log down on it. After a set of slams it runs out of breath, and that is your window to hit it. Below half health it enrages: rocks rain down and Runners join the fight.

Boss: the Bog Brute

Waiting at the end of mission 10 in the Swamp. It guards a caged dog, and the cage opens when you beat it.

Ten missions, two regions

Forest

Missions 1–4 are rescues, and mission 5 is Clear the Forest. The zombie types show up one by one, ending with the Log Brute.

Swamp

Missions 6–9 are rescues, and mission 10 is Drain the Swamp. Expect Spitters, sunken ruins, a bog graveyard and a drowned village, and the Bog Brute at the end.

Your village: jobs, upgrades and the forge

A walled village with a gate, farm, river, lumber yard, mine, forge, restaurant and market stalls. Villagers work, eat and sleep on a 24-hour clock and dress for their job.

Jobs

FarmerFood.

CookMakes food last longer.
BlacksmithCrafts items at the forge.
SellerCoins (at most 6 sellers).
GuardMans a watchtower at night.
TraderTrades at the trading post: 2 swaps a day per trader level.

Every villager eats food and drinks water each day.

Upgrades

  • Villagers gain XP by working. Promote them to level 2 or 3 for coins and tool kits, and stars appear over their heads.
  • Buildings: each of the 7 work buildings upgrades to level 3 for coins, wood, ore and building beams.
  • Output: every villager level above 1 adds 25%, and so does every building level, up to ×2.25 in total.

The forge

  • Upgrade yourself: Health, Belt damage, Stamina, Speed and Fart, paid for with coins, ore and wood.
  • Crafting: your blacksmiths make tool kits and building beams for village upgrades, plus the iron plate, spiked belt and running boots, which each boost your next mission only.
  • Building materials: nails, planks, hinges and chain, for the dog shed. Later they will also turn up in a new mode.
  • The forge's level sets how many items are made at once. More blacksmiths don't make things cheaper.

Beer party

Every 2 days at 19:00 the clock stops until you walk to the restaurant terrace. Then you get 20 seconds to tap as fast as you can and chug beers. Every beer you finish gives a random reward. If you're out on a mission at 19:00, the party waits for the next evening. It never overlaps a horde.

Trading post and shop

  • Trading post: next to the plaza. Its swaps turn one thing into another: coins or gems for ore, wood for ore, food for water and so on. Each Trader allows 2 swaps a day per level, and the Market's level adds to what you get.
  • Shop: open it from the main menu. It swaps gems for coins and coins for gems, and gems for tool kits, beams, building materials and stock. The village's wares unlock once the village does.

Rex and the dog shed

  • The rescue: Rex sits in a cage by the Bog Brute's lair on mission 10. Beat the boss and the cage opens. Rex follows you for the rest of the mission and bites zombies that come near you.
  • The shed: built next to the plaza in 6 parts: footing, floor, walls, roof, door and chain post, and bed and bowl. Each one costs materials, shows on the shed and makes Rex bite 10% harder.
  • Missions: once Rex is rescued and the shed is finished, he comes along on every mission. In the village he is always with you.

Fighting in the village

The joystick, attack button and fart button sit exactly where they do in the stages. Your health is the same as in missions (base, Health upgrade and iron plate), and the bar only shows during a horde.

The nightly horde

A horn sounds at 20:00 and the horde attacks at 21:00, every night. Until it's beaten, you can't leave the village.

It grows

5 zombies on the first night, 2 more for every night already fought, up to 30. They come in 2–3 groups, and their types and toughness follow your furthest completed mission.

New roads

They come from the west. The south road opens after 3 nights and the north road after 6.

Raids

Zombies always appear off screen on a road you can't see, then walk in. Ones that reach the stores at the crossroads rummage from stall to stall and steal food, water, wood and ore into a sack. After 3 thefts they run back out the way they came. Kill them to get it back, because one that escapes keeps what it took. At most a quarter of each stock can go in one night.

Clearing the night

Kill every zombie to clear the night. Villagers hide at home and guards climb their watchtowers. Zombies still there at 07:00 leave with what they carry.

If you go down

You wake at 07:00 with full health. Every zombie left takes one raid's worth, within the same cap. You never lose coins or gems.

Defenses

  • Barricades: place them from the Build panel. A ghost shows in front of you, green where it fits. Each costs 8 wood and has 150 health, and you can have up to 20. Zombies stop and hack at them. Stand next to one to repair it, or take it down for half the wood (not during a horde).
  • Watchtowers: build them on the 11 stone pads outside the wall, up to level 3. Each level adds health, range, damage and fire rate. A tower only shoots with a Guard on it, and a higher-level guard shoots harder. Zombies can break towers, and you repair them for wood.

How to install the APK on Android

The APK isn't on Google Play, so Android will ask for permission once.

  1. Download the APK with the button above, on your phone.
  2. Open the file when the download finishes. If Android blocks it, tap Settings and turn on Allow from this source for your browser.
  3. Go back and tap Install. If Play Protect warns you, choose Install anyway.
  4. Open The Fatty and play.

How to install The Fatty on Windows

The Windows version is a ZIP file. There is no installer: you unpack it and run the game.

  1. Download the ZIP with the Windows button above.
  2. Extract it first. Right-click the ZIP and choose Extract All. Don't run the game from inside the ZIP, it needs the other files next to it.
  3. Open the extracted folder and run The Fatty.exe.
  4. If Windows shows Windows protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway. Windows does this for games from small developers it doesn't know yet. Only do it for the file you downloaded from this page.

There is nothing to uninstall: to remove the game, delete its folder.
